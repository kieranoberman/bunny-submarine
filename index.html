Bunny's Flying Submarine

Up into the sky, down under the sea!

Who's playing?

Make your bunny nickname to start playing!

How to play: things float in from the right on different lines. Move the submarine onto their line to catch them. Press + to go up or − to go down, then a number, and off you go! Above 0 is the sky, below 0 is the sea. You have 2 minutes to catch as many as you can, and it gets faster as you go.
At level 9 the lines go up in halves (like 1.5 and −2.5), so there's a . key too.
Catch something with just one move for a Perfect! 3 points. Taking more moves gets 1 point.
On a keyboard: the + or − key, then a number key. Backspace if you pressed the wrong + or −.

About this game

Bunny's Flying Submarine was made by Kieran, a parent and maths enthusiast, to help children practise adding and taking away, including negative numbers.

What we keep:

  • Your made-up bunny nickname and your year group.
  • Your scores, so they can go on the leaderboard. The leaderboard resets every Monday.

What we don't keep: real names, email addresses, passwords, photos or locations. There are no adverts and nothing is shared with anyone else.

Your nickname is remembered on this device, so you don't need a password. If you choose to stay off the leaderboard, nothing is sent online at all.

Grown-ups: if you'd like to test the game (or just play!), feel free to sign up too. Choose "Grown-up" instead of a year group and you'll have your own leaderboard, separate from the children's.
